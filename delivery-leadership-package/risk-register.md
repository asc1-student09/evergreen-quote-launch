## How I'll use this register

I will re-read this register at each daily check-in and again before any merge or demo decision. It will be visible to the sponsor and the team as a live delivery artifact, not a document that sits untouched. The cadence is simple: review the top risks every morning, update likelihood and trigger status after any incident or deploy, and escalate any red item before it becomes a launch blocker.
